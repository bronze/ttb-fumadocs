## 1. What it is:

A Branding Idea gives the brand meaning, distinction and ultimately, fame in the consumer's eyes. A Branding Idea conveys the enduring emotional meaning of the Brand Vision to consumers though creative execution. It is at the heart of all communication briefs.

The Branding Idea distinguishes our promise to clients for a unique TOTAL BRANDING system. Whilst competitors may be competent at producing advertising ideas, J.Walter Thompson has long produced broader, campaignable ideas at the centre of communications.

The Branding Idea expands upon our acknowledged expertise, and offers a competitive edge.

The Branding Idea is composed of two elements; how the Communication works and the Creative Expression.

The first sets out how the communication seeks to bind the brand to the consumer. The more communication reflects how the brand meets the true needs and motives of consumers, the stronger and more enduring the connection.

For example the Brand Vision for Philadelphia, is "Permissible Indulgence". In the UK, the Philadelphia's Branding Idea connects this indulgence to the brand using the 'Worshipful obsession by Philadelphia lovers of its fresh taste and creamy texture'.

A Branding Idea's public face is the Creative Expression. The Creative Expression is what the consumer sees, hears or experiences. This is what ensures communication is distinctive - it is what brings the Idea to life. To do this, creative people often utilise existing or latent communication equities that are associated with the brand - the Branding Properties.

The Branding Properties Audit is a particularly useful tool at this stage to set out what communication equities may be draw upon. Only a handful can be used responsibly for any brand through its Communication Plan. These properties are tools that can be used in appropriate media vehicles. All of them can not be used every time in every media.

Consumers may come to identify a Branding Idea through the Creative Expression and the properties communications employ. However, Creative Expression is only the executional part of the Branding Idea, and should not be mistaken for full Branding Idea.
