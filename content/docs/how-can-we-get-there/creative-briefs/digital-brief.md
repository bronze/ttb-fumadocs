---
title: "Digital Brief"
---

What is the digital vehicle that we must create? How does it relate to other marketing activities?

What kind of vehicle is it? An internet site - or just one part of a site? A button or banner to be placed at other sites on the net? A CD-ROM disk? A floppy disk? An interactive kiosk? An interactive TV spot? Specify the size and depth of this vehicle.

The key points to cover are:

- What is required?
- What do we expect this vehicle to achieve?
- How will success be measured?
- Who is the audience?
- What response do we want this vehicle to elicit?
- What is the best evidence to support this response?
- What aspects of the brand's personality are important here?
- What is the branding idea and how does it work?
- What initial creative ideas might be worth exploring?
