## What is Sales Promotion?

Sales promotion focuses on the building of share growth and brand loyalty through the strategic use of long-term and/or a series of short-term programs that change the existing price/value relationship.

The key features to cover are:

- What is required?
- What do we want this promotion to achieve?
- How will the result be evaluated?
- Who is our key audience and what have we discovered that is helpful about their behaviour, beliefs or feelings?
- What is the key benefit to enhance?
- What evidence in the brand could be helpful in emphasisng this?
- What aspect of the brand's Personality is important to this promotion?
- If there is a Branding Idea, what is it and how does it work? Are there any other useful Branding Properties?
