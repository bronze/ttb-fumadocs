---
title: "Direct Brief"
---

Again, the most important audience for a direct marketing brief is the creative team. For them a good brief is:

**Clear, coherent**... It should flow like a good story and be in simple English.

**Single-minded**... It should be precise in its purpose ie a direct call to action or strengthening of relationship bonds.

**Insightful**... It should contain real news or facts that are helpful in producing the desired response/action.

**Ambitious**... It should be the stepping-stone to an exceptional program idea.

The key points to cover are:

- What is required?
- What do we expect this direct activity to achieve?
- What will be the measure of success for this program?
- Who is the audience and what have we discovered about their behaviour, beliefs & feelings that is helpful?
- What is the key benefit we wish to enhance?
- What evidence in the brand could help in emphasising this?
- What is the biggest hurdle to response?
- What evidence in the brand could help in overcoming this?
- What aspects of a brand's personality are important?
- What initial ideas may be worth exploring? If a brand idea exists, what is it and how does it work? What other branding properties could be used? (attach any helpful video/pictures)
