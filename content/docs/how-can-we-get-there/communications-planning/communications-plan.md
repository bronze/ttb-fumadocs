title: "The Communications Plan"
---

## How to do it:

- Reprise target (primary or secondary) communications objective. (If more than one target, complete separate forms.)
- Record which vehicles you will use by checking the "recommended" boxes and record the communications task this vehicle will help to accomplish.
- Record timing implications, and estimated budget (either as a $ amount or % of resources). Review the form and delete any redundancy.
- Establish a priority for each task, confirm that the highest priority tasks can be funded under the overall budget.
- If resources are limited, reduce the number of tasks before cutting programs in higher priority tasks.

How will we evaluate the effectiveness of this vehicle? Clients expect accountability. Planning for such metrics in advance ensures their timely development and implementation.
