## What its for:

This exercise helps to develop the specifics of how we will achieve the communications objective. It recognises that to achieve an objective, several "steps" or activities might be required. These are referred to as Communications Tasks.

## How to do it:

Reprise a concise statement of the communications objective.

If you do not have a clearly defined communications objective, do this now.

Reprise the target market to ensure our thinking is focused against the right group of customer.

Break the communications objective into the specific communications tasks whose combined effects allow you to achieve the objective.

Now, using the consumer buying system, customer relationship analyses or other information sources, develop a "laundry list" of all the contact points throughout the customers buying and relationship stages where the target customer and persuasive communications can intersect.

Then, consider what you know about the target consumer. Where and how you can reach then during key contact opportunities?

## Further details

### Concise Statement of the Communications Objective

**Reprise a concise statement of the communications objective**

If you do not have a clearly defined communications objective, do this now.

Some advice on the communications objective:

Think of the communications objective as an organizing statement. It is an important intermediate step between the client's marketing objective and individual communications tasks. It provides a singular direction to align all forms of communications. Proper use of the communications objectives should allow you to:

- Allocate budgets correctly
- Avoid competing channel strategies
- Talk/channel alignment
- Cause channels to work together towards common goal

### Target Market: the Right Group of Customers

**Reprise the target market to ensure our thinking is focused against the right group of customers.**

If more than one target, complete separate opportunity analyses for each.

### Specific Communications Tasks

**Break the communications objective into specific communications tasks**

The tasks combined effects allow you to achieve you objective.

A simple example:

**Objective:**

Lose 50 pounds over the next 12 months.

**Tasks:**

- Exercise 4 times/week.
- Limit calories to 1500/day.
- Cut fat intake to 20% of total calories.

### Opportunities for Contact with Target Customer

**Now, using the consumer buying system, customer relationship analyses or other information sources, develop a "laundry list" of all the contact points throughout the customers buying and relationship stages where the target customer and persuasive communications can intersect.**

What do our consumer buying system, customer relationship analyses or other experiences tell us about critical "contact points" where we can use communications to impact behaviour?

What contact points and communications tasks synch up?

What specific communications could you implement at a contact point to help reach the communications objective?

Narrow your list to the opportunities with highest potential.

### Key Contact Opportunities

**Now, consider what you know about the target customer. Where and how can you reach them during key contact opportunities?**

In addition to their media habits, are there affiliations or leisure activities which provide a special venue? Are your customers databased, providing an opportunity to reach them one to one?

Mass media advertising is only one of these possible intersections. Selling messages communicated through a variety of disciples creates greater synergy and multiplies the power of persuasion.

If you need help determining the "right tools for the job", work through the *Communications Evaluation Grid* as a reminder of each tool's stength and weaknesses.
