---
title: "Research Preceding Creative Briefs"
---

## What it is:

Research can play a role in isolating the right language, emotional and rational, visual and verbal, to express a promise to consumers by a brand.

This can be used to help a brief become more focused, more creative, more of a springboard for ideas. The use of adcepts, probing the right language for a brand, is one invaluable tool, as are non-directive interviewing techniques, such as projection, anthropromorphisation, role playing, etc. to go beneath superficial responses.

## What its for:

To help create the most precise, useful and creative, creative briefs based upon an understanding of how to express consumer motivations for a brand.

## How to do it:

Select the precise target audience with whom you wish to communicate.

Recruit a sample for qualitative research; structure the qualitative research in a way which will maximise the usefulness of response; for example, use groups to develop a creative workshop atmosphere, depth interviews for probing individual feelings, pairs to dicover the key interplays between key consumers (husbands and wives, best friends, etc).

Try to do the research in an enabling environment, if possible; for example, do airline research in an airport terminal, corporate research in a boardroom, washing powder research in a laundry, etc. Create a playful, creative atmosphere. Get respondents to act out their feelings, rather than only respond to questions; tap into their creativity. Make them think of 10 ways to do something, rather than respond with the one or two ways they do actually something.

Prepare some creative stimulus for respondents to use. Adcepts, either in a print or audio-visual form, can be used effectively to help develop the right creative language. Adcepts should not be used to find a 'winner' per se, but to stimulate response. Empower the respondents to respond to stimuli in a creative way; perhaps hand the respondents the adcepts you produced, and ask them to pretend that they created them in their capacity as 'marketing people'; have them present their recommendations to you as the 'client.'

To create adcepts, 'walk around' the agreed positoning. For example, a brand of potato crisps may be positioned as offering 'Irresistable taste' - but this can be expressed as unstoppable eating, obsession, secret eating, etc. Express all the ways you can think about the positioning and put them in adcept form.

Finally, try to do this work yourself, whenever possible; it will make you more insightful, powerful and authoritative. Always include the core team to observe this kind of research. They will find it useful and stimulating.
