---
title: "Creative Development Research Guidelines"
---

There are two forms of creative development research that are useful additions to the process of developing outstanding communication ideas; qualitative research to isolate the most creative expression of a brand's offering to consumers, and thus more powerful briefs, and qualitative research designed to help identify and strengthen the best creative expressions or scripts.

The following guidance and tools may help.

- [Research Preceding Creative Briefs](./research-preceding-briefs.md)
- [Creative Development Research](./creative-development-research.md)
- [Creative Research Checklist](./research-checklist.md)
