---
title: "Creative Research Checklist"
---

- Who should respond most to the communication?
- How should their response be shaped in terms of what they understand about the brand, how they feel about the brand, and what their relationship may become?
- Does the communication set out to:
  - Persuade the consumer,
  - Involve the consumer in the communication,
  - Get the attention of the consumer to reconsider the brand?

- How does the communication try to achieve a change in how the consumer views the brand?
- How does the communication idea tap into consumer thoughts, feelings and motives?
- What language is most effective for the brand in tapping into consumer thoughts, feelings and motives?
- How can this idea be optimised to communicate powerfully?
