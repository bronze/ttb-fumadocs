Ideas rarely come out perfectly formed the first time. The creative process is one of creativity and refinement. The target for communcations is always the consumer, not ourselves or our clients; if the consumer does not respond correctly, we must try again.

## What it is:

Creative Development Research is the best way to take one or more fledgling creative ideas, and to identify what is strong about them and what needs strengthening. It is qualitatative and diagnostic, not quantitative and evaluative in nature. Clients may choose to use the latter form of research to 'test' ideas; that is their choice. We need research to help build robust, effective creative communication.

## What its for:

To explore how creative ideas work in order to identify how they are working and to suggest how they can be improved.

## How to do it:

Qualitative research with the core target audiences is the preferred way of completing this kind of research. Many of the thoughts provided about completing research to develop better briefs apply here; do the research with the appropriate sample in an enabling, creative environment, and avoid judgemental interviewing techniques.
