---
title: "SWOT Analysis"
---

A SWOT Analysis is a long established tool to analyse a brand's positioning.

- What are the brand's key strengths?
- Why is the brand successful?
- What tools does the brand have in its possession?
- What are the brand's weaknesses?
- What attributes does it lack?
- Does it have brand values?
- Does it meet a real consumer need?
- What Opportunities face the brand?
- What positive attributes, currently under-utilised, can it bring to bear?
- What category trends does it fit?
- What can truly distinguish this brand?
- What Threats does the brand face?
- What aspects threaten the brand today; category trends, competitor's strengths, client production issues, etc?
- What future threats does it face?
