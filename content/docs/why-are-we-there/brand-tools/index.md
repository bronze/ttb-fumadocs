## What it is:

A set of tools to help understand and experiment with scenarios concerning your brand's position, so you can identify the key attributes which need to change to make the brand more competitive.

## What its for:

The purpose is to encourage you to identify what's really important for the brand and its competitive position and to discover the changes you need to make in terms of brand positioning.

## How to do it:

Firstly, review the brand's [current situation](../../where-are-we-now/brand-assessor-checklist/current-brand.md) from the previous section and the Brand Assessor Research. This should enable you to begin to understand what the brand is all about, and what challenges it faces.

Secondly, set down your insights on the [SWOT Analysis](./swot-analysis.md).

Thirdly, set down potential scenarios using the [Brand Map](./brand-map.md) tool.
