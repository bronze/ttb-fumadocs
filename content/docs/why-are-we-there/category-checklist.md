---
title: "Category and Market Discoveries Checklist"
---

## What it is:

A prompted list of questions you should address about what is happening in the market overall and your brand's category, that your brand may tap into or exploit. These could be trends in consumption, pricing, regulation etc., which are shaping the market and category.

## What its for:

The purpose is to prompt insights into how your brand may benefit from changing its competitive position by better meeting category and market trends.

## How to do it:

Simply answer each question by consulting all available data sources; industry journals, market research statistics and qualitative information. A notebook page will enable you to briefly set down your discoveries to the checklist questions.
