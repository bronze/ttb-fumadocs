title: "Branding Properties Audit"
---

Here are some examples that might help you understand what we are talking about.

1. Is there a design element within the logo that is exploitable?

2. Is there any vocabulary associated with the brand?

3. Is there an action or some body language associated with the brand?

4. Is there a particular spokesperson associated with the brand (even historically)?

5. Is there any music or jingle intrinsically linked to the brand ?

6. Is there a character (real or fictional) associated with the brand?

7. Is there a way of using the product that is distinctive/ownable?

8. Is there a way to show the product in a distinctive/ownable way?

9. Are there any particular mnemonics or devices associated with the brand name?

10. Is there a product demonstration that has been particularly effective?

11. What are the core products associated with the brand?

12. Are there any promotional activities which have contributed to the brand's image?

13. Is there any aspect of the service (including the staff) that is recognised/distinguishing?

14. What direct marketing and loyalty schemes does the brand own?

15. Is there any sponsorship the brand owns?

16. What are the key design elements that brand the identity?

17. What specific colours or combinations are associated with the brand?

18. Is there any particular packaging associated with the brand name?

19. Is there anything in the real estate of the brand that is distinctive or ownable?

20. What about the brand's mythology or heritage? Who invented it? Who has used it? Where has it been used? Any urban myths/jokes about it?

Once you have done your Brand Properties Audit, the team then needs to understand why those things are (possibly) significant or important. Three key questions need to be asked:

- What is this property or vehicle or perspective saying to consumers about the brand? What is the response this stimulus is eliciting from the consumer?
- How is it doing that? How is it working? How could it work?
- Is it (still) relevant? Why? Why not? Could it be relevant?

## Examples

### 1. Logo

Is there a design element within the logo that is exploitable?

e.g. the Nike flash, the Boots lozenge, the Disprin/Dettol sword.

### 2. Vocabulary

Is there any vocabulary associated with the brand?

e.g. "Have a break" for Kit Kat,

or "Built Ford Tough"

or "Hit the Hut" for Pizza Hut

or "We try harder" for Avis

or "You'll know when you've been Tango'ed"

or "Finger Lickin' Good" for KFC,

"Schhh.....you know who" for Schweppes,

"Exceedingly good cakes" from Mr Kipling,

"Snap crackle pop" is synonymous with Rice Krispies,

the" Tangy Zip of Miracle Whip",

"Brainfreeze" scream for 7/Eleven Slurpee frozen drinks,

"Chuck E. Cheese please" as kid request,

Kellogg's Corn Flakes "Sunshine Breakfast",

"Who's next?" for Super Lotto,

Will's Filter tip cigarettes "made for each other".

### 3. Action & Body Language

Is there an action or some bodylanguage associated with the brand?

e.g. The Oxo cube crumble gesture of rubbing your thumb and first two fingers tgether,

the Nescafe bean shake in a closed fist, bending your ear to listen to Rice Krispies,

Tony the Tiger punching the air as he says "They're Grrreat",

flapping your arms like a chicken when you "feel like Chicken Tonight",

the Remegel tablet squeeze.

### 4. Spokesperson

Is there a particular spokesperson associated with the brand (even historically)?

e.g. OJ Simpson for Hertz, Candice Bergen for Sprint,

Ted Moult for Everest Double Glazing,

Paul Hogan for Fosters,

George Hamilton for Philadelphia

### 5. Music or Jingle

Is there any music or jingle intrinsically linked to the brand ?

e.g. De Beers Vivaldi soundalike,

"Mellow Birds will make you smile" jingle,

Lakme's Flower Duet for British Airways,

"Oh I wish I were an Oscar Mayer Weiner..." for Oscar Mayer hot dogs.

### 7. Using

e.g. The Orangina bottle shake,

KFC finger licking,

Autodrop in car candy.

What specific colours or combinations are associated with the brand?

e.g. Esso - red & white,

### 18. Packaging

Is there any particular packaging associated with the brand name?

e.g. the Grolsch bottle, the Kenco jar.

Is there anything in the packaging that lends itself to other communications areas?

e.g. Lucozade's orange bottle,

Schweppes bubbles,

Gordon's Gin green.

### 19. Real Estate

Is there anything in the real estate of the brand that is distinctive or ownable?

e.g. The IKEA store path,

the PanAM sorry MetLife building,

the OXO tower,

The Sears tower,

Cathay Pacific Marco Polo lounges,

the Harrods building.

### 20. Mythology

What about the brand's mythology or heritage? Who invented it? Who has used it? Where has it been used? Any urban myths/jokes about it?

e.g. Mick Jagger and Marianne Faithful and Mars Bars,

Scott and the Antarctic and Kendall Mint Cake,

Tom Jones and Listerine,

OJ and the white Ford Bronco,

The 7th Day Adventists who opened a sanatorium in Battle Creek to preach ideas on health reform,

Henry Ford "any colour you like as long as its black",

Bonnie and Clyde and Fords.
