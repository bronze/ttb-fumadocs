## What it is:

Having completed the investigation on the category, brand, client, consumer and communications, it may help focus your thoughts to set them down on the summary sheet provided.

In the course of your work, you may have come to conclusions about what trends you want your brand to exploit, what changes are required for your brand's positioning, what aspects of consumer behaviour and attitudes are vital, what elements of the communication mix are worth developing, and what aspects of the client's dreams and aspirations for the brand, or what attributes of the product are vital to its future; it is at this point in the process that you should set down your hypotheses.

While it is not mandatory to discover the need for change in every aspect listed, it may be worthwhile to consider each aspect separately and then as a cohesive whole. Additionally, there may be more than one possible scenario to develop for a brand. Setting these down will help you clarify your thoughts and help you develop the right priorities.

## What its for:

The summary will provide the focus of your thinking for the Day One Brandstorm; it will act as a summary for the participants in the session who are less familiar with these issues.

## How to do it:

Revisit each section of 'Why are we there?' and write down those aspects you wish to develop for the future of the brand, discarding those you may wish to scrap. Think of your goals for the brand and how you can achieve them.
