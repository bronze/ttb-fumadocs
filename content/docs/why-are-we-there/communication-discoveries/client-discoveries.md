title: "Client Discoveries Checklist"
---

## What it is:

A simple checklist of some of the key client considerations that should form a basis for future planning.

## What its for:

A key part of discovering a brand's potential is to reveal exactly what the brand owner's ambitions are and what resources can be employed to achieve this. This can range from quite deep-seated personal aspirations, to financial considerations, to technical resources that can be utilised, to product service developments planned and many more.

## How to do it:

This will clearly depend on the nature of the client and of the relationship with the agency. Experience has taught us that the higher the level of trust that exists, the closer the feeling of partnership and the more willing the client is to open up both business and personal ambitions and resources.

This is clearly best lead by the Director in Charge and tailored to match the client. The checklist is merely a list of topics that have been helpful as a reminder.

## Checklist:

- What financial considerations are important (such as profitability/return on investment/investment levels)?
- How is the Brand overall seen by client? ( e.g. Boston Grid Star/Problem Child/ Cash Cow...)
- What is the Brand's relationship with other portfolio brands? How important is it and what priority does it command?
- What level of risk is acceptable if the potential reward is high?
- What is the client willing to change to develop the brand? What is felt to be inviolate?
- What technical, development or production resources can be leveraged? What advantages or drawbacks does the client feel the brand has against key competitors?
- What are the clients feelings about the brand? How will its development affect the client personally?
- What does he/she feel is the core of the brand? Are there aspects of the brand's history that are felt to be important?
