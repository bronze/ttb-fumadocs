---
title: "External Influencers"
---

While a brand may use communications well, have a single positioning and use each vehicle correctly, all may be lost if journalists disagree with the positioning, retailers fail to support it and the industry is critical.

For example, it was difficult for Nestle to position dry cat food as a complete diet after stories in the media revealed that cats fed this way could die of kidney failure. It is difficult to command a premium price if retailers don't support that positioning. As you can see, sometimes these external influencers can undermine an entire communications programme.

Search through the media, trade and specialist journals and industry periodicals and ask:

- How do they view the category?
- How do they view the brand?
- Do they support or undermine the positioning?
- Can you exploit positive views?
