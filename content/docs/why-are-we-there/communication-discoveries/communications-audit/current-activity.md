---
title: "Current Activity"
---

The Current Activity worksheet sets out all possible mediums in the current communication mix and asks, by vehicle:

- Who is the core target audience?
- What role does this vehicle play in the mix?
- How much money is spent on this activity?

It may be necessary for clients to help you fill this out, as agencies aren't always privileged to know this data. If your team finds it impossible to obtained detailed figures, make a best estimate.
