## What it is:

Brands communicate to consumers through all of their activities. The Communication Audit tool presents a simple but very comprehensive way of assessing all of the communications for a brand; those within control of the brand owner, those external influences out of the brand owner's control (such as journalists, distributors and retailers); and the Semiotic or cultural signals the brand sends out, whether planned or accidental.

After auditing all intended and unintended stimulus, the Audit is completed with CommAssessor Research, a specially designed research tool aimed at gauging the success of the communication with the core target audience. From this we can begin to assess the whole current Communication Plan, and suggest what changes are desirable.

## What its for:

The purpose of the Communications Audit is to discover how the brand is using communications now, how successful the overall plan and elements are in the marketplace and what changes might benefit the brand.

## How to do it:

The first step is to assess [Current Activity](./current-activity.md).

Next, fill in The [Evaluation of Current Activity](./evaluation-summary.md).

The third step is to examine response to the brand by key [External Influencers](./external-influencers.md), like the media, those who distribute and sell the brand and the industry in which it competes. After assessing these stimuli, we turn to consumer response.

To help, we have developed a proprietary primary research tool called [CommAssessor Research](./commassessor-research.md).
