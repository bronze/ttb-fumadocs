## What it is
CommAssessor Research is a tool designed to assess consumer awareness and response to all of the brand's controlled communications in a quantitative interview.

## What its for
Using all available stimuli, it enables you to understand what response each communication vehicle creates with core target consumers.

## How to do it
The agency has developed a special software programme which enables respondents to see all forms of stimuli - branded and unbranded, on a specially prepared P.C. In Europe, this service will be provided by Network Research, based in the U.K..

Firstly, identify the core audience to be interviewed. They should be the primary target audience of the brand. A minimum of 150 respondents should be interviewed. Interviews are conducted face to face and are interactive with the computer.

Secondly, select the stimulus. This should include all stimulus used by the brand:

- Name/pack
- Advertising by medium (competitors as well)
- Direct Marketing
- Sales promotion, etc, as relevant

You may assess each vehicle, branded and unbranded, if you so wish.

Thirdly, complete the survey by ascertaining:

- Awareness of the vehicle.
- What the key consumer response to each vehicle is.
- How positively/negatively consumers respond to the vehicle.

Network or other research suppliers will work with you on developing the questionnaire.

Fourthly, CommAssessor takes time and costs money.

- It takes 3-4 weeks to complete. It costs £40 per average interview (U.K./Europe) - (£8,000 + V.A.T. + preparation costs for 200 respondents).

CommAssessor Research can offer a competitive advantage, so place it high on your importance list if you want to understand fully how the brand's communications are working today.
