The first element is brand positioning. Does the brand present the consumer with a single, consistent overall positioning? Are the vehicles used all pulling in the same direction? If not, why not? A brand stands for something, or it is nothing but a product badly targeted.

The second element asks for proof in the success of the media vehicle used. What results can the vehicle claim? Look for market research data, sales, enquiries etc to ascertain whether or not the activity is working. How successful is the activity as a whole?

Lastly, please put down your thoughts on how the vehicles could be used to better effect. What are the overall opportunities for improvement?
