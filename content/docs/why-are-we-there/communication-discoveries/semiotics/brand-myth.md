title: "Semiotic Brand Myth"
---

Many successful brands reconcile fundamental oppositions and contradictions Myths.

## How to do it: Process to complete Myth Reconciliation

## Step 1: Determine Paradigm Sets

- Look through the codes you have identified, and determine how the meanings of each inter-link to form a paradigm (model of how things should be)
- These may, and probably do, cross socio-demographic and historical barriers, to form core 'meanings', all within the cultural class.
- Define the set of key paradigms - describe them in as much cultural detail as you can.

## Step 2: Myth Structures

- Work out the convention structures of how these codes are combined to become culturally expected paradigms.
- Organise the paradigms (and their associated conventions) into mutually exclusive sets.
- Map these onto Myth axis to explore the possible territories where the brand does or could resolve contradiction and thus become more meaningful.
- Observe the territories in your 'myths' and by observing the culturally unexpected and how this needs resolution - does this provide an opportunity for the brand?
- Build stimulus that expresses them for presentation/research.
