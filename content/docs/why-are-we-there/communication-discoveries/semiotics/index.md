## What it is:

An approach to building an understanding of a brand and the market in which it operates through an analysis of all its communications over time.

## What its for:

Often a brand's real meaning with consumers is only really understood if the unintended communications surrounding a brand are uncovered and if these are viewed in the light of the cultural communication codes that are prevalent. Semiotics enables us to study a brand's communications and shed light on how they have been changing and to give insight into how they could move on given the way that overall communication language and meaning is changing.

There are two key semiotic principles:

- Codes - Many successful brands have their own 'codes' that have common cultural meanings. **Semiotic CODES**
- Myths - Many successful brands reconcile fundamental oppositions and contradictions. **[Semiotic BRAND MYTH](./brand-myth.md)**

## How to do it:

You need to be trained in Semiotics to be expert. There is a Thompson Way of TOTAL BRANDING course on this. If you have not yet attended this then you should try and employ someone who is trained and use the following method:

- Review all the brand's communication overtime, and that of its key competition.
- Analysis the dominant, residual and emergent codes of your brand and the market in which it operates.
- Consider the myth that your brand attempts to resolve.
- Complete the attached semiotic analysis templates for your brand.
