---
title: "Trigger"
---

At the Trigger Stage, we are examining what triggers initial interest in the category.

There are several different ways to start off the process:

- It could be routine - running out of detergent, need to replenish.
- It could be the need to solve a problem that exists - your current car has come to the end of its life.
- It could be an indulgence or treat - from ice cream to a vacation.

Here we are concerned with the category, and identifying the kind of purchase it is.

It is helpful to examine what the consumer is doing, thinking and feeling at this (and any other) stage.

Contact opportunities could be advertising, POS, Public Relations (PR), direct mail, packaging.
