---
title: "Considering"
---

At the Considering Stage, the consumer thinks about what the purchase is needed for, i.e., what they want out of it.

Again concentrating on the category, and looking at the needs a purchase can satisfy,

Ask...

- Is this purchase for self-maintenance? For example: shampoo, foodstuffs, - goods of a routine nature, where little consideration is required.
- Is this purchase for self-expression? For example: clothes, cars, cigarettes, cosmetics.
- Is the purchase for self-reward/gift? For example: jewellery, cosmetics, books.
- Is the purchase for self-improvement? For example: books, computers, investments, sports equipment.
