---
title: "Experiencing"
---

At the Experiencing Stage, the consumer is using the brand, and asking whether the product lived up to their expectations.

Here we are determining how we can either...

- enhance the consumer's experience with the brand, and deepen the relationship, or
- return the consumer to the beginning of the CBS and trigger another purchase.

With a - the deepening of the consumer-brand relationship, we are usually talking of services and durables, for example, banks, telecommunications, airlines, hotels, cars, etc.

If we are in such categories, another useful tool is the *Customer Relationship System*.
