---
title: "Searching"
---

At the Searching Stage, the consumer asks what are the best available sources of information, since they are beginning to evaluate the brands available in the category.

The consumer may use information based on their own experience, or the experience of their friends and family (word-of-mouth).

They may turn to information in newspapers, magazines and/or Consumer Reports, or to communications vehicles under our control, such as advertising, direct mail, PR, brochures, POS, etc.
