---
title: "Acquiring"
---

At the Acquiring Stage, the consumer actually "buys" or takes action.

There are several factors which can affect selection of our brand:

- Basic distribution and display (i.e., availability) are key to many purchases whether the purchase is a cereal brand on a shelf, or a car in a showroom.
- Promotions can be influential here, as can special pricing strategies.
- Free trials and giveaways can also prompt purchase by proving what the brand is like.
- Salespeople may have a role in closing the sale.
- And finally, for many brands which have little substantial differentiation, after-sales service guarantees may persuade the consumer.
