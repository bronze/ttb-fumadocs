---
title: "Choosing"
---

At the Choosing Stage, the consumer examines brand differences and compares what is available in order for them to be able to make their choice.

Broadly speaking, both functional, physical values and non-functional, emotional values will influence choice.

We are examining which values separate the brand from the competition (for example, price, performance, trust, brand personality, etc.).

Brand advertising as a communication vehicle is obviously key here, but other vehicles can also play a significant role in elevating the brand's advantages.
