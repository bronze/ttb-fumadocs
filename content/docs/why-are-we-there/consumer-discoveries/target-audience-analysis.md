---
title: "Target Audience Analysis"
---

## What it is:

It is necessary to define the target audiences important for the brand today, and for the foreseeable future. The Target Audience analysis asks you to consider who these audiences may be and how important they are to the brand.

## What its for:

Identifying the key focus of future communication activity.

## How to do it:

First, consider the target audiences you might address.

As you consider them, write down a definition which is useful to the brand. For example, on Jaguar, the target audiences are defined in two central ways: Loyal Purchasers and Conquest Customers.

Loyal Purchasers are men aged 45+, who are self starting, entrepreneurial businessmen who either own their own company or practice or are senior company directors. They are current owners who have also previously owned a Jaguar, and use it as their primary car for work. They love Jaguars, and have put up with the quality problems which used to affect the marque.

Conquest Customers are defined as buyers of BMW and Mercedes Benz cars, aged 35+. They may have owned a Jaguar in the past and went to German cars because of better perceived quality, reliability and technology. Younger buyers may never have bought anything else. They are likely to question Jaguar's technical credentials. They have gone away from buying British, but long to return, if the quality is right. We call them 'fond rejectors', and the younger ones, 'fond non-considerers'.

The next question is, how important are they currently as users? What percentage share do they account for? Are they heavy users of the product or category ? What do they contribute to the brand in terms of profit?

Sometimes the current situation is undesirable or warrants change. In the next column, set down which groups you believe will be important for the future of the brand.

In the case of Jaguar, the bulk of current sales comes from the Loyalists. However, with changes to quality and the model line up, the Conquest Customer will become the priority, if the marque is to meet ambitious sales targets. Advertising will have to reach out to these Conquest Customers, whilst direct marketing will play a leading role against Loyalists. So in column two, the current situation may be 85%/15% in favour of Loyalists, the future target will be 60%/40% in favour of Conquest.

The Target Audience analysis is the tool you can use to prompt thinking about with whom you should develop an understanding of buying behavior and customer relationship, so that you can effectively communicate.
