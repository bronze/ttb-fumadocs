The Introduction Stage is actually the acquisition stage, where the customer "buys" the brand. It can be considered as the sum of the first five stages of the CBS, from Trigger to Acquiring.

The customer is asking "Why should I buy you/What can you do for me?"

If we have already investigated a Buying System our brand, we can summarize the findings and the most relevant key contact points here.
