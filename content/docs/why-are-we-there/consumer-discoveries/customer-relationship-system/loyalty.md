---
title: "Loyalty"
---


At the Loyalty Stage, we are looking to retain the customer in the long term, and ensure that we have a strong emotional bond with the customer. Here we are giving the customer a reason to stay, either emotionally, or rationally, e.g.,

- A long-distance company's discounts increase each year the customer stays.

- The Discover card gives money back at the end of the year.

The customer should feel rewarded and taken care of, such that they become an advocate for the brand, recommending it to friends and acquaintances

Often the most powerful bonds are created when there is a guenuine reason and means for a dialoque with the custmer on a one to one basis. Today's media make this a more viable route.
