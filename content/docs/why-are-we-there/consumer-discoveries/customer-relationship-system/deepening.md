---
title: "Deepening"
---


At the Deepening Stage, the customer is asking of the brand, "What else do you have for me?" or "How can you make my life easier?" Essentially, the customer wants to try more products and services from the brand they have come to value and trust. Here is an opportunity for the brand to deepen its relationship with the customer by cross-selling.

For example, a long-distance phone company may also offer, apart from a basic plan, services such as phonecards, pagers, cellular, even entertainment services such as cable television.

An airline may offer phone calls from the plane, computer hook-ups, video services, etc.

A car company, such as Ford Motor Company, may form a bond with the customer through one brand (for example, an Escort) such that when that same customer wants another type of vehicle, such as a sport utility, the customer is more inclined to choose a Ford Explorer.
