---
title: "Appreciating"
---


Once we have a satisfied customer using our brand, we may want to encourage them to use and value our brand more, (and thereby also encourage them to remain loyal).

This could take the form of -

- incentive programs, such as frequent flyer awards on airlines, frequent traveler awards at hotels, etc.

- actual product enhancements, such as being credited for spending more (with credit cards), "tiered" long-distance plans whereby the more you spend the more you save, buy 10 Lotto numbers, get one free, etc.

- service enhancements/bonuses, such as free servicing for your car/stereo/washing machine after a certain amount of usage or time has elapsed.

The customer is asking of the brand, "Are there other ways/occasions I could use your product?"
