---
title: "BrandCepts Research"
---

BrandCepts should be researched qualitatively with the key target audiences for the brand, in depth interviews, kinship Pairs, mini-groups or full group discussions.

Thought should be given to the ideal place for the research, so that it can be completed in a creative atmosphere (e.g. an airport lounge for frequent travellers, a bar for beer drinkers, etc.)

The research should operate as a creative workshop with consumers, rather than a stimulus/response research atmosphere. Use of projective techniques, role playing and other facilitation techniques should enable consumers to be creative and constructive, not reactive and judgmental.

The objective is to get the consumer input which will help identify and focus, the Brand Vision.

BrandCept research should be carried out by trained agency personnel or nominated researchers. The strategic nature of the research should make this less sensitive for clients than, for example, researching creative executions.

The team which worked together on Day One, should be involved in this research; the discoveries should be shared and discussed throughout the process.

The team that completes the research will own the outcome - the Brand Vision.
