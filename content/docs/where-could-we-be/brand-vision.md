---
title: "Brand Vision"
---

## What it is:
The summation of the essence or soul of the brand, expressed in a few words and visualised using video footage or static pictures. It should also include a summary of the brand's appeal to consumers, defining its distinctive benefits and how it should connect to consumer.

## What its for:
To become the stategic focus of the brand's communication plans, acting as a template for all subsequent creative development. If a Branding Idea doesn't exist for the brand, the Brand Vision should be the focus of generating one.

## How to do it:
The Brand Vision should be the summation of the strategic development process. Following Day One and the optional BrandCept Research, the Brand Vision is the strategic output which defines where we want the brand to be.

Defining the essence of the brand in a few words, the Brand Vision should be a product of rigorous strategic thinking. Planners have found it useful to express this vision as a paradox faced by the consumer, resolved by the brand, which is inherently oxymoronic.

For example, Kraft Singles - Playful Nutrition.

Children like to play with snack food, and like to pull apart, roll up or eat Kraft Singles in their hand. So it offers playfulness to consumers. At the same time, cheese is a nutritious food mother's prefer their children to eat, in preference to less healthy foods; thus the nutrition in the Vision. Kraft Singles resolves the paradox of a food which is playful (children's need) to one which is nutritious (mother's need and product offering).

## Brand Vision Focus:
When thinking about the focus of your Brand Vision, it may help you to think in these bi-polar, or oxymoronic terms. Brands tend to meet consumer needs through the product in a unique way. This resolution forms the strong connection between the consumer and the brand in a strategic, long term sense. It may help clarify your thing to express the ultimate promise of the brand using this device.

In defining the brand's appeal, you will be reframing, in new language and thoughts, the Current Brand Situation statement. Once again, this is expressed in human terms, as if the brand was an individual, not a product or service. Using this kind of language helps you think of the brand as a living, organic being, not an abstraction or dead object. Simply answer each question posed based upon the essence of this relationship. If no relevant expression of how the brand is known exists, don't hesitate to say so. Remember, the key use of the Brand Vision will be to define the future strategic direction for the brand.

## Visualisations:
It is suggested that you attempt to visualise the essence of the Vision, using video footage and music, or static visuals. The purpose of this is to help generate agreement with and excitement for, the direction of the Vision among all the key participants in the process.

Press your examples key for some successful Visualisations (in TTB software)

If you are putting together a video, here are some useful hints. If you need help, involve your creative team and your account handlers. Try to use borrowed visuals from ads, television, films and pop music videos which help express your Vision. Pick an appropriate music track to add emphasis, but only one which clearly makes the correct strategic point. If you need to title the Visualisation with words, you can add captions. Above all, the Visualisation should capture the emotional essence behind the Vision.
