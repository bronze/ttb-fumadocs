The Day One structure is based on James Webb Young's "Technique for Producing Ideas" (the original Structure diagram was not archived):

## Gathering

(Before the day) Day One assumes that the basic Brand Assets and Key Discoveries Analysis (Where are we and why?) has been completed prior to the session, using relevant Thompson Way templates. Properly completing this analysis may take some time if primary research is involved. It is the responsibility of the Planning and Account Service Departments.

## Immersion (morning)

The morning of the Day One session begins with a definition of the problem/objective of the session, followed by an immersion of the whole group into Key Discoveries about The Category, The Consumer, The Brand, Communications, and the Client. The Immersion should be engaging and provocative, not simply a chart-deck presentation or data dump. Customer videotapes, buying system analysis and brand mapping are all relevant tools for the immersion. The Immersion is meant to encourage an open-minded exchange of ideas, past experiences and personal prejudices to stimulate and focus the team. Beginning thoughts on the strategy will undoubtedly emerge and can be discussed.

## Break Out and Birth (lunch through mid-afternoon)

Following the Immersion, the large group is broken into several smaller multi-disciplinary teams of 4 or 5 people each. Each Breakout Group is charged with completing a Strategy Worksheet (more about the output in the next section).

## Presentation and Refinement (mid-afternoon through close)

The large group reconvenes and each break-out team presents their recommendation. These recommendations are discussed and refinements are made. If there is a strong consensus, it is possible that a single strategic direction may emerge. If not, there may be several alternate hypotheses for further research.

## Incubation and the Morning After (24 to 48 hours after close)

After a day or two to incubate and work individually, the Core Team (not the entire Day One group) meets to make final recommendations - either on a specific Brand Vision and strategy or on branding concepts ("BrandCepts") for consumer testing.
