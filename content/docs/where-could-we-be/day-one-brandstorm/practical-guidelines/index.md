## A Final Note

While these are useful guidelines, there's no one absolutely "right" way to conduct a Day One. It is an inherently fluid and dynamic process and every one is unique. The only essential ingredients are a group of open-minded dedicated people from different disciplines, a concentrated amount of time, a structured agenda that alternates between large group and small group exercises, and a simple, concise format for summarizing their workproduct. These basic ingredients will virtually guarantee that you will leave the session feeling that the whole team has a much better understanding of the Brand's situation and has made significant progress towards a strategic solution.
