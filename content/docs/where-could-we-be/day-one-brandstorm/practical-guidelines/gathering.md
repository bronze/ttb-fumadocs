---
title: "Gathering: Preparation for the Session"
---

It's obviously important to understand the client's business, to know what client information exists, and how and where to find additional information from outside sources or consumer research.

It's also important to be able to boil things down to their essence and bring a strong customer orientation (rather than a marketing orientation) to the session.

Therefore, Planning and Account Service people take the lead in finding and synthesizing existing information and, if necessary and possible, gathering additional information.
