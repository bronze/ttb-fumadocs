---
title: "The Rooms"
---

The Immersion Room needs to be large enough for the whole group to be comfortably seated and have room to move around. If possible, a single consistent location should be used for all Day Ones to help streamline the process. A focal point is needed for presentation of Immersion materials...an overhead projector and screen or large display pad. A video player and monitor will obviously be required for consumer videos or category television advertising. Two or more large pads and markers should be available for taking notes during the session. These notes should be pinned or taped up on the walls of the room as stimulus for the group during the discussion.

It's desirable to dress or decorate the room in a way that's appropriate to the category and brand being discussed. The goal is to create a mood to help participants put themselves in the shoes of consumers when they are buying or using our clients' products.

Logos, category advertising, pictures or collages may be appropriate. If possible, a retail display case might be simulated. If the product is tangible (as opposed to a "intangible" like a service) it should be available for participants to interact with. It may be desirable to simulate the usage environment. For example, in a Day One for a shredded pizza cheese product the Immersion room was decorated like a pizzeria, with checkered tablecloths and candles in wine bottles. In a bacon Day One, client and competitive products were prepared in the room to compare shrinkage and taste. (The aroma wafted through the whole agency, making everyone's mouth water.) The point is, it helps to develop a creative environment to generate a creative output. The Immersion needs to be experiential, not intellectual. Use your imagination.

While it's inevitable that phones and faxes need to be accessible, the group should be as isolated as possible from the distractions of day-to-day business. There should certainly be no phone in the Immersion room itself.

Break-Out Rooms will also be required for the smaller Break-Out teams to work on their Strategy Workplan. Usually one team can work in the Immersion room and the other teams will require separate locations. Each room should be equipped with a supply of worksheets as stimulus and large pads and markers to collect the team's thoughts and present their ideas to the whole group.
