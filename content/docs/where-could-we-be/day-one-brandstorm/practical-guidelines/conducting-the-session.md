---
title: "Conducting the Session"
---

**Several skills are required for a successful Day One Session:**

- Good presentation skills to keep the Immersion sessions lively and interesting. 
- Good group dynamic skills to keep the discussion moving and on track, and to help keep any one person (such as a senior client) from dominating. 
- Good judgement about what's important to the Big Picture and what's not, to set priorities and make decisions between alternative directions or issues for consideration.

In light of these needs, the senior Planner is typically responsible for moderating or "facilitating" the session with assistance and occasional guidance from the senior Account person. However, any combination of Core Team people best suited to the task can share this responsibility.
