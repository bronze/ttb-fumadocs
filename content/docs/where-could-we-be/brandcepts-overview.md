---
title: "BrandCepts Overview"
---

A BrandCept is a creative expression of a consumer positioning which focuses on a key aspect the target consumer might find motivating in communications.

A product of the Day One process, they are developed by the core team for testing in consumer research.

BrandCepts are a primary stimulus to help evoke the Brand Vision with consumer input and feedback.

BrandCepts work best when they have a clear focus.

A potential consumer thought, feeling or benefit, expressed in creative language (a simple metaphor or demonstration), should be at the heart of each BrandCept.

The number of BrandCepts one produces depends on how many distinctions in consumer thought and creative language you wish to explore.

Don't expect BrandCepts to hold the solution; remember they are stimulus.
