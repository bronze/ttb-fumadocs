---
title: Involvement
---

Involvement of the target consumer with our Brand may be measured by degree of interest in the product or service category, brand preference or loyalty, or specific brand "relationship" attributes exploring relevance, credibility or competitiveness.

These measures are typically obtained via quantitative surveys of target consumers usually conducted as a series of "tracking" measurement to monitor changes over time. In general, we can use two sorts of "involvement" measures: General measures like familiarity, relevance, value for the money, likeability, etc., and Category specific measures like taste, nutrition etc., for a food brand, performance, economy, etc. for an automobile or sound clarity, ease of use, etc. for a telecommunications product.
