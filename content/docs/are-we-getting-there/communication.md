---
title: Communication
---

Two types of measures are used to evaluate impact and clarity of communications: Awareness or salience of the communication (Do target consumers remember the brand? The communication itself?) and communications understanding or meaning (can target consumers play back the message on an unprompted basis or with the aid of agree-disagree statements?)

Again, these types of measures are typically obtained from quantitative target consumer "tracking" surveys. In fact, Communications and Involvement evaluative measures may often be included in the same survey.
