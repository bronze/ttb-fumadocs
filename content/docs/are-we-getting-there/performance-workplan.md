---
title: Performance Evaluation Workplan
---

This workplan will help structure a Total Branding Communications Evaluation Program. A different workplan will be needed for each key target audience, which should be indicated at the top of the page. Keep in mind that the target audience might include consumers, distributors, retailers or purchase influencers.

For each target audience, indicate which Action objectives are relevant (Communication and Involvement are always relevant.) For each objective, indicate the Communication Vehicle(s) aimed at that objective and the Measurement Tool(s) used to monitor progress. The "Specific Response Desired" and "Quantitative Goal" refer to the intended message or image we intend to convey, the behavior we wish to stimulate and the specific criteria or levels we expect to reach. These will obviously be unique for every Brand Communications Program: What awareness level do we expect to achieve? What specific Brand message do we intend to communicate? What image attributes do we expect to change? How do we expect to change penetration, frequency or occasion of use?

It is desirable to complete this Workplan before the Communications Program is launched, and, if possible even before creative and media development are initiated. A clear sense of how program success will be measured can help shape a more effective effort.

The Workplan also obviously stands as a reference point against which actual results can be compared.
