---
title: Performance Evaluation Checklist
---

## Objective: Communication

**Measurements**

- Brand Awareness - unaided/aided
- Communications awareness - unaided/aided
- 'Media' Awareness: Adv., promo, PR, Event, Design, Change
- Message playback - unaided/aided

**Research tools**

- Quantitative In-Market Tracking Survey
- JWT ComAssessor
- Custom 'Forced Exposure' Communication Research

## Objective: Involvement

**Measurements**

- Category interest/involvement
- Stated brand purchase intentions
- Brand Image/Attitude shift
  - General
  - Category specific

**Research tools**

- Quantitative In-Market Tracking Survey
- JWT Brand Assessor
- Custom 'Forced Exposure' Communication Research

## Objective: Action

**Measurements**

- Telephone inquiries/requests
- Package/label reading
- Showroom/retail traffic

**Research tools**

- Client/Database sources
- On-site observation
  - In-store (purchase related)
  - In-home (usage related)
- Quantitative In-Market Tracking Survey
- DM program

## Objective: Seek Information

**Measurements**

- Reported Information Search
- DM response measures

**Research tools**

- Client/Database sources
- On-site observation
  - In-store (purchase related)
  - In-home (usage related)
- Quantitative In-Market Tracking Survey
- DM program

## Objective: Purchase

**Measurements**

- Penetration/Buying Frequency
- 'Aggregate' sales or share change
- 'Disaggregate' (individual) sales response
- DM response measures
- Sales Promotion response

**Research tools**

- Quantitative In-Market Tracking Survey/Diary Panel
- Client sales/share data/Syndicated Sales Measures
- Single Source Scanner Data
- DM program
- Sales Promotion program

## Objective: Use

**Measurements**

- Frequency/occasion of use
- Trial
- Repeat purchases

**Research tools**

- Quantitative In-Market Tracking Survey
- Custom 'Diary' Panel
